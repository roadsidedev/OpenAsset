# Getting started

Use the OpenAsset app to borrow stablecoins against collateral or supply stablecoin liquidity to an isolated market.

## Connect your wallet

1. Open the app and connect a supported wallet.
2. Switch to the chain your target market lives on. Each chain has its own Factory and Registry, and markets are per chain.

## Pick your action

| Goal | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Borrow stablecoins against what you hold | [Borrow](/docs/use/borrow) |
| Earn from stablecoin liquidity in a specific market | [Supply liquidity](/docs/use/supply) |
| Launch a market with your own terms | [Create a market](/docs/curate/create-market) |

## Before your first transaction

- **Markets are isolated.** Your collateral and the LP's liquidity live in one market contract. One market's bad debt can never touch another market.
- **Terms are set by the market creator.** Review LTV, APR, duration, grace period, oracle, liquidation path, position type, and adapter trust badges before you sign.
- **Eligibility is checked first.** If a market has a Compliance Adapter, the app checks eligibility before prompting wallet approval, so ineligible users do not spend gas on a revert.
- **Permissionless is not risk-free.** Read [Scope and risks](/docs/security/scope-and-risks) before depositing collateral or liquidity.

## Next step

Borrowers continue to [Borrow](/docs/use/borrow). Liquidity providers continue to [Supply liquidity](/docs/use/supply). If no market fits your asset, [create one](/docs/curate/create-market).
