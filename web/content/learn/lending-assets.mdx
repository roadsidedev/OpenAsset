# Lending asset scope

## v1 rule

**The lending (borrowed-out) asset is stablecoins only.**

This is a Factory-enforced allowlist, not LP discretion. Borrowers receive stablecoin proceeds and may convert outside the protocol.

## Why

Adapter-ization is scoped to the **collateral** side first. Expanding pluggable-asset logic to both sides of a loan simultaneously multiplies audit surface for limited near-term benefit.

## Future

Non-stablecoin lending assets (ETH, BTC) are an explicit, demand-gated future item — see [Deferred / out of scope](/docs/security/deferred).
